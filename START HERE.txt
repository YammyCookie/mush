MUSH - YOUR PHOTOGRAPHY WEBSITE
==============================

ADD PHOTOS
1. Copy JPEG or PNG photos into the "Photo inbox" folder beside this file.
2. For collections, create subfolders inside Photo inbox, e.g. Travel or Street.
3. Double-click "Publish website.cmd".
4. Keep the window open until it says "Uploaded successfully".
5. Visit https://yammycookie.github.io/mush/ after a few minutes.

Publish only photographs you want visitors to see. The website is public.
Keep the Photo inbox on your computer; it is excluded from GitHub uploads.
Your original photos are never edited. Display copies are JPEGs up to 2400
pixels on their longest edge; thumbnails are up to 1000 pixels. Display
copies do not retain the original EXIF/GPS metadata. A photo's filename
becomes its caption. Rename it in Photo inbox to change the caption.

Supported input: JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIF, TIFF.
For HEIC or RAW files, export a JPEG from your photo editor first.
Make sure OneDrive has downloaded the photos before publishing.

CHECK WITHOUT UPLOADING
Double-click "Prepare photos.cmd". This updates the local gallery only.

IF THE SHORTCUT CANNOT UPLOAD
Open GitHub Desktop, choose mush, review the changes, type a short message,
click "Commit to main", then "Push origin". The photos must have been
prepared first using one of the shortcuts above. On first use, you may
need to sign into GitHub or set your name/email in Desktop's Git options.

FIRST-TIME HOSTING SETTINGS
At https://github.com/YammyCookie/mush/settings/pages choose:
Source: Deploy from a branch
Branch: main
Folder: / (root)
Save. You do not need a paid plan or a custom domain.

REMOVING PHOTOS
Moving a photo out of Photo inbox and publishing hides it from the gallery.
Previously uploaded display files are retained in GitHub, including its
version history. For complete removal, ask for help cleaning those files
and their history. This shortcut does not delete files from your computer.

KEEP AN EYE ON SPACE
The shortcut reports the size of uploaded display copies. GitHub Pages
allows a published site up to 1 GB. Old display copies also use space.
Keep a separate backup of your originals.

FOLDER CONTENTS
Photo inbox       Your original photos (local only)
photos            Generated display copies for the public website
data              Gallery index, generated by the shortcut
assets            Website appearance and gallery interactions
scripts           Photo preparation and publishing helper
index.html        Website homepage

The gallery has collection filters, shuffle, fullscreen viewing, arrow-key
navigation, Escape to close, and swipe navigation on touchscreens.
